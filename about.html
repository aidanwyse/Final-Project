About Us

We are Sammy’s Bar & Grill, a local favorite in Huntley, Illinois where great food, cold drinks, and good times come together.

Whether you're here for burgers, wings, pizza, or just to catch the game with friends, we pride ourselves on delivering a laid-back atmosphere with friendly service and a welcoming vibe.

From family dinners to weekend nights out, Sammy’s is the place where locals gather, relax, and enjoy the moment.
